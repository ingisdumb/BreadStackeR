# Game Overview

## Title
BreadStackeR

## Concept
BreadStackeR is a simulation game where you stack bread onto carts and load it onto a truck that delivers it to clients. The warehouse is vast and echoey, also dark, darker, yet darker. Bread is loaded into the warehouse from a factory adjacent to the warehouse.

## Targeted Aesthetics
Fantasy, Submission, Narrative

## Game Flow
Players receive a receipt, they receive a tutorial and are given instructions on how to do their job.

## Player Controls
WASD to move - E to interact - F for flash, V for night vision, right click for dark light


# Mechanics


## Rules
The goal of this game is to survive 5 or more nights as you pack bread into trucks, but with enemies constantly watching you.

## Physics
Player cannot jump (basically glued to the floor)

## Movement
Up, down, left, right, and sprinting
	
## Actions + Abilities
Certain objects (i.e: shelves and walls) will prevent the player from moving to a certain area.

## States
Losing: death means losing
Winning: beating the level means winning
Level-Up: Spend coins gained in level to gain extra skills.

## Core Game Loop
Player receives receipt at the beginning of the night, and they go package the necessary items into the truck. The player can choose after every night to work overtime or go home.


If overtime = player’s hidden risk level goes up, and the possibility of them becoming victims arises. 

If home = the player will sleep, risk level is set to zero, and the next night begins with no additional money earned.

## Level Design
A vast empty warehouse,
Shelves placed strategically per level, and
**The void.**
